# Lumen & Pine — brand kit (synthetic, for benchmarks)

Lumen & Pine is a fictional design studio used only in benchmark briefs.

## Colors
- Ink: #1F2A2E (text, headings)
- Pine: #2F5D50 (primary — buttons, links, one accent per screen)
- Paper: #F7F4EE (page background)
- Clay: #C8754B (highlights only — at most one per slide or section)
- Mist: #DCE3E0 (borders, dividers)

## Type
- Headings: "Fraunces", serif, weight 600
- Body: "Inter", sans-serif, 16px base, 1.55 line height
- Numbers in tables: tabular figures

## Voice
Calm, specific, no hype. Short sentences. Say what changed and why it matters.
Never use exclamation marks.

## Logo
Use `logo.svg` on light backgrounds, top-left, at least 24px tall. Do not recolor it.
