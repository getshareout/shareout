# Changelog

All notable changes to the public ShareOut tree are documented here.
Format based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

Two parts: **Release notes** — what changed for people who use ShareOut, newest first, in plain
words, each with its Spanish line and a short note for integrators (the docs site renders it as
*What's new* / *Novedades*) — and **Unreleased**, the detailed technical log. A user-visible PR
adds to both (AGENTS.md → Gate contract).

## Release notes

### 2026-11-21

- **Send the ShareOut team an idea or feedback from your AI.** Tell Claude, ChatGPT or any AI connected to ShareOut "send ShareOut the idea: …" and it reaches the team, which reads every one. Questions work the same way. The AI may also send a note on its own when ShareOut got in its way, about ShareOut only and without your personal or company data, and it tells you when it does.
  - es: **Mandale al equipo de ShareOut una idea o un comentario desde tu IA.** Decile a Claude, ChatGPT o cualquier IA conectada a ShareOut "mandale a ShareOut la idea: …" y le llega al equipo, que los lee todos. Las preguntas funcionan igual. La IA también puede mandar una nota por su cuenta cuando ShareOut le complicó algo, solo sobre ShareOut y sin tus datos personales ni los de tu empresa, y te avisa cuando lo hace.
  - Guide: /everyone/connect-ai-apps/#ideas-and-feedback
  - Guía: /es/everyone/connect-ai-apps/#ideas-y-comentarios
  - Integrators: `POST /v1/support/tickets` takes `category` `feature_request` and `feedback` (with `question`); its `message` then starts with "Thanks". New MCP tool `send_feedback` (`kind`, `summary`, `details`, `artifact_id`, `language`). Ideas, feedback and questions sent by an agent are capped at 10 an hour per account across REST (API token) and MCP (`429 RATE_LIMIT_EXCEEDED`); problem tickets and questions typed in the app are not. Instance admins filter the support queue by these categories (`/admin?view=support`, `GET /v1/support/tickets?scope=all&category=feature_request`).

- **View counts add up again.** Views of your artifacts were recorded but never added to the daily totals, so Analytics, This week on Home and the view counts on artifacts stopped growing, and today's number could include older views. Views count again from now on. Views older than two days that were never counted can't be brought back.
  - es: **Las vistas vuelven a sumarse.** Las vistas de tus artefactos se registraban pero nunca se sumaban a los totales del día, así que Analítica, Esta semana en Inicio y las vistas de cada artefacto dejaron de crecer, y el número de hoy podía incluir vistas viejas. Desde ahora se vuelven a contar. Las vistas de hace más de dos días que nunca se contaron no se pueden recuperar.
  - Integrators: `analytics_events.created_at` is ISO text and is now always compared with ISO strings (the daily rollup, `GET /v1/home/analytics` and the per-artifact analytics, the instance admin metrics and digest); unix seconds compared as text, so the rollup summed nothing and "today" counted every raw event. `totals.activeArtifacts` now includes artifacts opened today. The rollup also re-counts, by itself, any day of the last week it marked done with nothing counted while that day's raw events still exist.

- **Home fits what matters on one screen.** A greeting with how many things wait on you, a box to ask your workspace or make something (Table from an Excel file, Upload files), and cards: Your artifacts as small tiles with a preview (Recent, For you, Favorites, Shared with me), Needs you with the button each item asks for (older than two weeks stays in the bell), In your workspace, This week (views of what you made, against last week) and What's new in ShareOut. All your artifacts sits below as a table. On a phone, Needs you comes first and the tiles slide sideways. Customize still reorders and resizes the cards.
  - es: **Inicio entra en una pantalla con lo que importa.** Un saludo con cuántas cosas te esperan, una caja para pedirle algo a tu espacio o crear un artefacto (Tabla desde un Excel, Subir archivos), y tarjetas: Tus artefactos en mosaicos chicos con vista previa (Recientes, Para vos, Favoritos, Compartidos conmigo), Te necesita con el botón que pide cada cosa (lo de más de dos semanas queda en la campana), En tu espacio, Esta semana (vistas de lo que hiciste, contra la semana anterior) y Novedades de ShareOut. Todos tus artefactos queda abajo, en una tabla. En el celular, Te necesita va primero y los mosaicos se deslizan de costado. Personalizar sigue reordenando y cambiando el tamaño de las tarjetas.
  - Guide: /everyone/your-workspace/#home
  - Guía: /es/everyone/your-workspace/#inicio
  - Integrators: `GET /v1/home/for-you` and `GET /v1/home/recent` add `miniHtml` (small tiles); `GET /v1/home/browser?mini=1` adds it too (first 12). Home's This week reads `GET /v1/home/analytics?range=7`. What's new comes from these release notes at build time (`npm run build:whats-new` → `src/pages/home/whats-new-data.ts`; `check:changelog` fails when it is stale).

- **Opening a page shows a clean bar on top instead of a menu full of buttons.** The page's name sits on the left, with who owns it and which version you're looking at; on the right, a favorite star, Schedule, Comments and Share. Comments opens in a panel that floats on the right, so the page stays in view while you write. The rest (Follow a number, Skills) is in a menu under the page's name. Editing, stats and settings now live only in ShareOut: owners get Open in ShareOut in that menu, and can turn the bar off from Details → More → ShareOut bar. The bar also shows on phones.
  - es: **Al abrir un artefacto ves una barra prolija arriba en vez de un menú lleno de botones.** A la izquierda, el nombre del artefacto, de quién es y qué versión estás viendo; a la derecha, una estrella de favoritos, Programar, Comentarios y Compartir. Comentarios se abre en un panel que flota a la derecha, así el artefacto sigue a la vista mientras escribís. Lo demás (Seguir un número, Habilidades) está en un menú debajo del nombre. Editar, estadísticas y configuración ahora están solo en ShareOut: el dueño tiene Abrir en ShareOut en ese menú, y puede apagar la barra desde Detalles → Más → Barra de ShareOut. La barra también se ve en el celular.
  - Guide: /everyone/share-it/
  - Guía: /es/everyone/share-it/
  - Integrators: `_viewer_config.show_on_mobile` is gone (the header always shows on phones); `hide_toolbar` still hides it. The viewer's floating-toolbar DOM (`#shareout-admin-toolbar`, `#so-toolbar-trigger`, `#so-back-home`) is replaced by `#so-header`.
- **Messages opens right away.** Your conversations show the moment you open Messages instead of sitting on Loading…, and the rest of ShareOut no longer stalls for seconds on a slow sign-in check.
  - es: **Mensajes abre al instante.** Tus conversaciones aparecen apenas abrís Mensajes en vez de quedarse en Cargando…, y el resto de ShareOut ya no se traba varios segundos por un chequeo de sesión lento.
  - Integrators: the session check reads its KV cache with the 500 ms budget (`kvCacheGet`) and fills it after the response (`deferWork`); Home's boot request is `GET …/messages?limit=50` (was `?unread=1&limit=20`).

### 2026-11-19

- **Write to someone in your workspace without leaving ShareOut.** A new Messages menu in each workspace holds one-to-one conversations with the people on your team: pick someone with New message, write, press Enter. Unread messages show on the menu and in the bell, and the other person also gets a note on Telegram if they connected it. You can ask the assistant (on Home, Telegram or WhatsApp) or your own AI connected to ShareOut to send one for you — it shows who it goes to and the exact text first, nothing is sent until you confirm, and the message is marked as written through your assistant. Only workspace members can message each other; clients you share folders with are not included.
  - es: **Escribile a alguien de tu espacio sin salir de ShareOut.** Un menú nuevo, Mensajes, en cada espacio guarda conversaciones de a dos con la gente de tu equipo: elegís a alguien con Nuevo mensaje, escribís y Enter. Los mensajes sin leer se ven en el menú y en la campanita, y a la otra persona además le llega un aviso por Telegram si lo conectó. Podés pedirle al asistente (en Home, Telegram o WhatsApp) o a tu propia IA conectada a ShareOut que mande uno por vos: primero te muestra a quién va y el texto exacto, no se manda nada hasta que confirmes, y el mensaje sale marcado como escrito con tu asistente. Solo los miembros del espacio se pueden escribir; los clientes con los que compartís carpetas no están incluidos.

  - Guía: /es/teams/messages/

Los asistentes de Telegram y WhatsApp ahora trabajan siempre dentro de un espacio del que sos parte, así lo que cuestan cae en ese espacio, y el reporte de IA tiene un nuevo desglose Por canal (ShareOut, Telegram, WhatsApp).

- **Turning Files into pages never makes the same page twice, and owners can find and clear copies.** If the conversion of Files into pages is run again while a batch is still going (a slow scan, a timeout and a retry), each file still becomes one page. Workspace owners can list pages made from the exact same file — matched by its content, so two different documents with the same name are never mixed up — and move the copies they pick to the trash, where they stay recoverable for 30 days.
  - es: **Convertir Archivos en páginas nunca crea la misma página dos veces, y el dueño puede encontrar y quitar copias.** Si la conversión de Archivos a páginas se vuelve a lanzar mientras una tanda sigue en curso (un escaneo lento, un corte y un reintento), cada archivo sigue dando una sola página. El dueño del espacio puede ver las páginas hechas del mismo archivo — comparando el contenido, así dos documentos distintos con el mismo nombre nunca se confunden — y mandar a la papelera las copias que elija, recuperables durante 30 días.

### Security

- **Backfilled 2026-11-18:** page metadata is escaped in the shell, quick-edit frames run no scripts and the create preview is sandboxed (#218); capture renders use narrower credentials and never reach private networks (#241).

- **Tenant isolation hardening:** workspace Agent and external (`sot_`) tokens are accepted only by the artifact, publish, data and support routes that enforce their workspace and scopes (`403 INSUFFICIENT_SCOPE` elsewhere); session cookies accept only session-shaped tokens and honour the disabled flag on the data plane too; admin-issued member tokens are limited to service accounts, and revoking tokens also revokes connected-app (MCP) grants; `POST /v1/auth/link-email` now requires the verification-code flow; connector OAuth `state` is signed and bound to the installing admin (artifact-level installs to the owner); per-table write roles in the page's `shareout/manifest` are enforced (an unreadable declaration allows only the owner); external members, disabled and disabled-linked accounts no longer count as internal on home, search, skill context, trash, inbox, Telegram/Slack links, scheduled jobs and crew runs; file, collection and deliverable actions are scoped to the caller's bucket; moving an artifact needs its owner, an editor or a workspace admin; viewers' Slack DMs go only to their own linked account; caches for connector results, service-account tokens, Snowflake JWTs, grants and idempotent publishes are keyed per tenant and caller; closed artifact bytes are `private, no-store` on every origin and artifact documents served on the app origin are sandboxed; anonymous abuse reports apply only to public artifacts; the email-events webhook requires its secret. New guard tests require every route and MCP tool to refuse anonymous/foreign callers or carry an explicit public reason.

- **Data Platform row-level scope is enforced structurally (skill v2.58.0):** under an `access_policy`, a scoped viewer can no longer send SQL to a platform connector. They run a query the author declares in the new `access_policy.queries` (`{ connection, provider, endpoint, params }`) by name (`execute(endpoint, { query: '<name>' })`); the server binds the viewer's values into `:viewer_scope` — counted only in SQL code, never in comments or quoted text — or, when the SQL has no placeholder, wraps it as `SELECT * FROM (<sql>) AS so_scoped WHERE so_scoped.<field> IN (…)`. Declared SQL must be one statement (checked when the policy is saved); anything that cannot be scoped returns `403 SCOPE_REQUIRED`.
