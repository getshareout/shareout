# Voice & Tone

We speak like a smart friend.

Not a consultant. Not a salesperson. Not a corporation.

---

## Core Belief

**Publishing should feel like a conversation, not a control panel.**

We build tools that disappear. The interface gets out of the way so people can focus on what matters: their content, their ideas, their audience.

---

## Voice Traits

- Friendly human, not corporate robot
- Confident helper, not desperate salesperson
- Brief and clear, not clever and confusing
- Human, brief, confident helper

---

## Good

- Publish in one click.
- Build anything.
- Share it with everyone.
- Your idea, live on the web.
- Share your page with anyone.

---

## Bad

- Leverage our platform.
- Unlock synergies.
- Empower stakeholders.
- Deploy digital assets.
- Maximize your content reach.

---

## Banned words

These are the AI-marketing tells. Never ship them: **Elevate · Seamless · Unleash · Next-Gen · Revolutionize · Game-changer · Delve · Leverage · Synergy · Empower · Supercharge.** Use a concrete verb instead ("Publish", "Share", "Build", "Edit").

No exclamation marks in success copy. Confident, not loud: "Page published." not "Page published!"

---

## Microcopy Rules

- Buttons say what they do: **"Publish Page"** not "Submit"
- No jargon: **"Share your page"** not "Deploy artifact"
- Errors explain AND suggest fixes, in plain language ("Couldn't publish. Check your connection?" not "Oops!")
- Empty states guide to next action
- Success messages are warm, not clinical
- Icons supplement labels — never icon-only for important actions
- Sample data is realistic: real-sounding names, organic numbers, no "John Doe" / "Acme" (see [../principles/anti_patterns.md](../principles/anti_patterns.md))

---

## Examples

| Context | Bad | Good |
|---------|-----|------|
| Marketing | "Leverage our platform to maximize your content reach!" | "Share your page with anyone." |
| Error | "An error occurred during the publication process." | "Couldn't publish. Check your connection?" |
| CTA | "Get Started" (vague) | "Create Page" (specific) |
| Settings | ⚙️ (icon only) | "Settings" (labeled) |

---

## Words First

Grandma doesn't know what ⚙️ means. She knows what "Settings" means.

- Labels on everything important
- Icons only as supplements, never alone
- Plain language, zero jargon
