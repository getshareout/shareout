# Brand Positioning

## One-liner

> Build anything you want in ShareOut, publish in one click, and share it with everyone.

## Expanded

ShareOut is where ideas become live, shareable experiences — instantly. Not a file folder. Not a slide deck exporter. Not a BI dashboard you configure for weeks. A single surface where imagination is the only limit.

**Key differentiator:** Agentic first. ShareOut is built for humans *and* AI agents to create, publish, and iterate. Modern and forward-looking without being cold or overly technical.

---

## What ShareOut Is

A dashboard. A pitch deck. A wedding RSVP. A finance tracker. A landing page. A custom internal tool.

Whatever someone imagines, ShareOut helps bring it to life and publish it instantly.

### Use cases span

- Business presentations and pitch decks
- Internal ops dashboards and BI-style reports
- Personal tools (finance trackers, planners)
- Life moments (wedding RSVP, event pages)
- Lightweight apps, each publishable and shareable

---

## What ShareOut Replaces

| Old stack | ShareOut replaces with |
|-----------|------------------------|
| Google Drive + Docs | Live, interactive pages |
| Google Slides | Presentations that are apps, not files |
| Tableau / Looker | Dashboards anyone can build and share |
| One-off micro-apps | Publishable artifacts in seconds |
| Scattered links and exports | One URL, always live |

*Positioning only — not legal claims.*

## Audience

### Primary

Creators · Builders · Founders · Consultants · Freelancers · Small businesses

People who want something live today.

- Individuals building for themselves (personal finance, events, hobbies)
- Entrepreneurs and small companies
- Creators who want to **put something out there** — fast, without friction

**They need to feel:** "I can do this in minutes" — not "I need IT approval."

### Secondary

Enterprise teams · Operations · Analytics · Internal tools · Client reporting · Executive communication

- Teams replacing BI tooling or the Google Drive + Slides stack
- Companies that want **one platform** for internal dashboards, client-facing reports, and shared artifacts
- Custom workspace subdomains (e.g. `acme.shareout.site`)

**They need to feel:** Capable and trustworthy — but still **not** Microsoft/legacy enterprise.

---

## Who ShareOut Is NOT For

- Teams that want heavy legacy BI with six-month rollouts
- Organizations married to the Microsoft/Google file-and-folder mental model
- Users who need deep offline-first document editing (Word replacement)

---

## Category Detail

Agentic Publishing Platform — a new category between:

- Documents
- Dashboards
- Websites
- Apps

ShareOut combines all four into a single publishing surface.

Most tools help people create files. ShareOut helps people create things that **live**.
