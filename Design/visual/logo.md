# Logo System

## Philosophy

The logo should communicate **possibility**.

Not sharing. Not uploading. Not exporting. **Possibility.**

Symbol-first identity — a mark that works alone at small sizes, paired with a wordmark for marketing and product header.

---

## Primary Concept: The Frame

An open frame representing possibility. A space where anything can exist.

The frame is never a box. Boxes contain. Frames invite.

**Meaning:** Anything can exist here.

---

## Secondary: The Portal

An opening. A transition. A passage from idea to reality.

**Meaning:** Go live.

---

## Third: The Transformation

A small shape evolving into a larger structure.

**Meaning:** Creation becoming reality.

---

## Symbol Meaning Flow

Create → Build → Publish → Live

---

## Logo Principles

- Simple enough to draw from memory
- Recognizable at favicon size (16×16)
- Distinct in monochrome
- Not dependent on gradients
- Not dependent on typography
- Not dependent on AI symbolism
- Can be drawn from memory
- Must survive monochrome and small-size reproduction

---

## Wordmark

**ShareOut** — CamelCase always.

Never SHAREOUT · Never Share Out · Never shareout.

Do not split into "Share Out" unless proven to read better at small sizes.

---

## Clear Space

Minimum clear space: **1× symbol width**. Nothing enters this zone.

---

## Minimum Sizes

| Context | Size |
|---------|------|
| Favicon | 16px |
| Navigation | 24px |
| App icon minimum | 32px |
| Marketing minimum | 48px |

---

## Lockups

| Type | Use |
|------|-----|
| Horizontal | Product header, marketing |
| Stacked | Social, square formats |
| Symbol-only | Favicon, app icon |

---

## Logo Color Usage

- Full color on light warm backgrounds (primary context)
- Single-color (blue or warm black) for print/constrained contexts
- Reversed (white symbol) on blue or dark backgrounds — WCAG AA minimum

---

## Conceptual Territories to Explore

Metaphors connecting to **create → publish → share**:

| Territory | Notes |
|-----------|-------|
| Outward / share | Signal leaving a container — broadcast, open door |
| Live / instant | One-click publish — pulse, "go live" |
| Canvas / creation | Blank page becoming something — frame, surface |
| Agentic / modern | Forward motion without robot clichés |
| Abstract & ownable | Like Figma's shapes — may not be literally descriptive |

**Avoid:** Chain links, cloud upload icons, document-with-fold corner, pie charts, robot/AI mascots.

---

## Shape Language (align with product UI)

- Rounded, friendly — product uses 12–16px border radius
- Soft, not blobby — warm and approachable, not childish
- Simple geometry — must survive monochrome at 16px

---

## Usage Contexts

| Context | Notes |
|---------|-------|
| Product header | ~20–24px wordmark + symbol |
| Favicon | 16×16, 32×32 — symbol must read |
| PWA app icon | 192×192, 512×512 |
| Marketing / hero | Full lockup, room for tagline |
| Open Graph | 1200×630 templates |
| Enterprise subdomains | Symbol + ShareOut or workspace co-branding |
| Email / docs | Single-color symbol acceptable |
