# Photography & Illustration

## Photography

Real people. Real builders. Real creators. Real workspaces.

Natural light. Warm environments.

### Avoid

Stock handshakes · Corporate meetings · People pointing at dashboards · Fake diversity photos · Overly polished startup clichés

---

## Illustration

Minimal · Geometric · Purposeful · Friendly · Abstract over literal.

Never cartoonish. Never mascot-based. Never AI robots.

---

## Motion (Brand Level)

Motion explains. Motion never decorates.

### Good Motion

Reveal · Transform · Expand · Fade · Guide attention

### Bad Motion

Bounce · Spin · Elastic effects · Attention-seeking animation · Anything that delays interaction

Brand animation (if any): subtle, skippable, never dizzy.
