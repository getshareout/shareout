**Messages** lets people in the same workspace write to each other, one to one, inside ShareOut. No channels, no new app to learn.

## Send a message

1. In your workspace, open **Messages** in the side menu.
2. Click **New message**, pick the person and write.
3. Press **Enter** to send (Shift + Enter for a new line).

The other person sees it in **Messages** and in the bell, and gets a note on Telegram if they connected it.

## From your assistant

Ask ShareOut's assistant (on Home or Telegram), or your own AI connected to ShareOut (Claude, ChatGPT):

> Tell Martín the October numbers are in, and link the sales dashboard.

It shows you who it goes to and the exact text first. Nothing is sent until you confirm. The message goes out in your name, marked "through their assistant".

Ask "did anyone write to me?" to read your messages.

## Who can I write to?

Anyone who is a member of your workspace. Clients you share folders with (external people) are not in Messages.
