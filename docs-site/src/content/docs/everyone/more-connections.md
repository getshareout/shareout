Beyond [Google Sheets](/everyone/google-sheets/), you can connect your **store**, your **analytics**, your team's **Slack**, and more.

## Your online store

Already selling on **Shopify** or **Tienda Nube**? Connect it and your page can show your real products — names, prices, photos, and what's in stock — pulled live from your store.

> *"Show my Shopify products in a grid, with price and a 'buy' button."*

Update a product in your store, and your page keeps up. Great for a landing page, a lookbook, or a "featured items" page that always matches your catalog.

## How many people are visiting

Connect **Google Analytics** and your page can show how it's doing — visitors today, this week, your most popular pages. Nice for a simple dashboard you share with a partner or team.

## Send updates to Slack

Connect your team's **Slack** and your page can post to a channel — a daily summary, a snapshot, or a heads-up when something happens. (Setting that up is in [Put it on autopilot](/everyone/automations/).)

## Almost any other tool

Have another web service you use? If it offers a way to pull data (most do), you can usually connect it too. You paste in the key it gives you, and your page can show its data.

> *"Pull the latest entries from my newsletter tool and list them."*

## Keep a backup on GitHub

Prefer to keep a copy of your page somewhere you control? You can export it to **GitHub** — handy as a backup or if a developer on your team wants the files.

## The secure part is handled

Whatever you connect, your keys and passwords are **encrypted and hidden** — never shown on your page or to visitors. You connect once; we keep it safe.
