Your page doesn't have to live on its own. You can **connect the tools you already use** — so your page shows live data from them, or sends things out to them.

## Two directions

- **Bring data in.** Show your products from your store, your numbers from a spreadsheet, your visitor stats — and keep them up to date automatically.
- **Send things out.** Post an update to Slack, email a report, ping a channel when something happens. (That's [Automations](/everyone/automations/).)

## What you can connect

| Connect… | …and your page can |
| --- | --- |
| **Google Sheets** | Show a list straight from your spreadsheet, always current |
| **Your online store** (Shopify, Tienda Nube) | Show your products, prices, and stock |
| **Google Analytics** | Show how many people are visiting |
| **Slack** | Send updates and reports to your team's channel |
| **Other tools** (any web service) | Pull in data from almost anywhere |

See [**Google Sheets**](/everyone/google-sheets/) and [**Your store & other tools**](/everyone/more-connections/) for the friendly walkthroughs.

## How connecting works

It's quick, and there's no code:

- For things like Google Sheets, you **sign in** to give permission — the same "Continue with Google" you've done a hundred times.
- For other tools, you paste in a key they give you (we'll point you to where it is).

Either way it takes a minute, and you only do it once.

## Your keys stay safe

Anything you connect is **locked away and encrypted** — your passwords and keys are never shown on your page or to your visitors. We handle the secure part so you don't have to think about it.
