A page feels like *yours* once it has your photos and the right pieces on it. Add them by asking, or by clicking to add.

## Your photos

Drop in your own pictures — your products, your space, your team, your event:

> *"Add a photo gallery of my cakes."*
>
> *"Put a big photo of the storefront at the top."*

Upload your images and they live right on the page — no separate photo hosting to set up.

## Sections that tell your story

Build the page out with the sections people expect:

- an **About** — who you are, in a sentence or two
- **Opening hours** or a schedule
- **Reviews** or testimonials
- an **FAQ**
- a **gallery** or portfolio

> *"Add an FAQ with three common questions, and a reviews section with three quotes."*

## Buttons, links, and a map

The little things that make a page useful:

- **Buttons** — *"add an 'Order on WhatsApp' button"* · *"add a 'Book now' button."*
- **Links** — to your socials, your shop, your latest post.
- **A map** — *"show a map of where we are."*

## More you can add

These have their own friendly guides:

- [**Collect replies**](/everyone/forms/) — add a form for messages, RSVPs, or orders.
- [**Lists & data**](/everyone/your-data/) — show a list of products, events, or prices.
- [**Add a smart assistant**](/everyone/assistant/) — a chat helper for your visitors.

## Not sure what your page needs?

Just describe who it's for and what you want them to do — *"this is for new customers, and I want them to book a table"* — and the assistant will suggest the pieces.
