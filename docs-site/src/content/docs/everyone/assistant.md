title: Add a smart assistant
description: Put a little chat helper on your page that answers visitors' questions from your own info.

You can add a friendly **chat helper** to your page — a little assistant your visitors can ask questions, that answers from *your* information.

It's like having someone on hand 24/7 who knows your menu, your prices, your FAQ, or your data — and replies in plain language.

## What people can ask it

It depends on what your page is about. For example:

- A café page → *"Are you open on Sundays?"* · *"Do you have anything gluten-free?"*
- A product page → *"What's the difference between these two?"* · *"Do you ship to Canada?"*
- A dashboard → *"Which product sold the most last month?"*
- An event page → *"Where exactly is the venue?"* · *"Is parking available?"*

It answers using the information on your page and the data you've added — so it stays on-topic and accurate.

## Add it

Just ask for it:

> *"Add a chat assistant in the corner that answers questions about my menu and hours."*

A little chat bubble appears on your page. You can set its greeting and where it sits.

## You're in charge of what it knows

- It answers **from your stuff** — your page, your lists, your data.
- You can tell it how to behave — *"be friendly and concise,"* or *"only answer questions about our services."*
- It's **off until you add it** — no surprise chatbot on a page that doesn't need one.

:::tip[Two different helpers]
There are two assistants in ShareOut: the one **you** chat with to build and change your page (see [Change anything](/everyone/the-editor/)), and this one — the helper you add **for your visitors**. This page is about the second.
:::
