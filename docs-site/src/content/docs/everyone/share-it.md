title: Share it

Every page has its own web address. Sharing is just sending that link — and you choose who gets to see it.

## Send the link anywhere

Copy your page's address and paste it wherever your people are:

- a text or WhatsApp message
- an email
- your Instagram or TikTok bio
- a poster with a QR code

Anyone you send it to can open it in their browser. Nothing to download.

## Decide who can see it

You're always in control of who can open your page. Tap **Share** on it, and under
**Who else can open it** pick one:

- **Only the people above** — just you and the people you invited. Nobody else can open
  it, even with the link. Good while you're still working on it.
- **Everyone in** *your workspace* — anyone on your team who signs in can open it. (Shown
  when the page belongs to a workspace.)
- **Anyone with the link** — no sign-in needed. Great for a shop, an invite, or a
  portfolio you want out in the world. (Shown only where your workspace allows public
  pages.)

You can change your mind any time — make a private page public when it's ready, or lock one back down.

### Invite people by email

1. Tap **Share** and type their email in **Add people by name or email**.
2. Want to say why? Tap **+ Add a message** and write a short note — it goes in their email.
3. Tap **Invite**.

They get an email with the link and sign in with a code we send them — no password, no
account to set up. Until they open it, they show as **Invited · hasn't signed in yet**.
If the email didn't go out (for example, you hit today's email limit), they still have
access: ShareOut tells you, and you can tap **Copy link** and send it to them yourself.

### If you can only view

You can still pass the link along: in **Share**, tap **Copy link**, or type an email under
**Email the link** and tap **Send**. Sending the link doesn't give access, though: only
people who can already open the page get in. To add someone, ask the page's owner.

## Show it once

For something confidential — new prices, a target, a notice for your branch managers —
you can give each person **the link plus their own code** instead of an invite:

- Each person gets **their own link** with the code already in it. They open it, tap
  **Open**, and see the page. **No account, no sign-in, nothing to type.** (The plain link
  plus the code works too, if you'd rather send them apart.)
- Each code works **once**. The page closes by itself after the time you chose (90 seconds
  unless you pick another), with no countdown on screen.
- Their **name is written across the page**, so any screenshot or photo shows who it came from.
- Copying, printing, saving and downloading are blocked.
- You see who opened it, when, on what device and browser, from roughly which city (never
  their IP), and what they tried (copy, print,
  leaving the page).

Ask an AI agent that uses the ShareOut skill (Claude Code, for example): *"Make this page
one-time for Ana, Luis and Marta, 90 seconds each"*. It gives you a ready-to-send message
for each person. The page people land on can carry your workspace's logo and color and your
own words ("Carta del socio fundador · Abrir la carta") — anyone with the link sees that page,
so keep the confidential part out of it. You can also turn on a **link preview**, so WhatsApp
or Slack show a cover picture, the title and the message instead of a bare link; it is off until
you turn it on, and the cover is public. Developers: [the API](/start/introduction/) (`/v1/artifacts/{id}/view-codes`).

:::caution[What no website can stop]
Nobody can block a screenshot, or someone taking a photo of the screen with another phone.
The name on the page is what makes that risky for them.
:::

## Ask for access

If someone sends you a link to a **private page** you're not on yet:

1. Sign in with an **email address** (Google or email code), so the owner knows who is asking.
2. Tap **Request access**.
3. The owner gets an email, and a notification in ShareOut Home (and on Telegram, if they
   use it). They can approve with one click.
4. You get an email when they answer. Once approved, the same link opens the page — no
   new link needed.

Can see a page but need to change it? Open it in ShareOut and tap **Request edit access**.
When the owner approves, you can edit it.

If you already asked, ShareOut shows that your request was sent — there's no need to ask
again. If nothing happens, message the owner directly.

## What people see when they open it

Your page fills the screen, with a slim bar on top:

- **On the left:** the page's name, and for people signed in, who owns it and which version they're looking at. The arrow goes back to ShareOut.
- **On the right:** a star to keep it in your favorites, **Schedule** to get it sent to you by email, Slack or Telegram, **Comments**, and **Share**.
- **Comments** opens a panel that floats on the right. The page stays visible, so you can keep reading while you write.
- Click the page's name for the rest: **Follow a number**, the page's **Skills**, and, if it's yours, **Open in ShareOut**, where you edit it, see its stats and change its settings.

Someone without an account sees only the name and, if comments are on, **Comments**. Want the page with nothing from ShareOut around it? In ShareOut, open the page, then **Details → More → ShareOut bar**, and turn it off.

## It looks good when you share it

Paste your link into a text, WhatsApp, or Slack and it shows a **nice preview** — a title, a description, and a picture — instead of a bare web address. ShareOut makes that preview for you automatically, so your page looks polished the moment you send it. Want a specific picture or wording? Just ask.

## Updates are instant

Changed something? Everyone who has the link sees the new version right away. Same address, always current — no need to re-send anything.

:::tip[A nice touch]
Print your link as a **QR code** for a table tent, a flyer, or a shop window. People point their phone and they're on your page.
:::
