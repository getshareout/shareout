Every page has its own web address. Sharing is just sending that link — and you choose who gets to see it.

## Send the link anywhere

Copy your page's address and paste it wherever your people are:

- a text or WhatsApp message
- an email
- your Instagram or TikTok bio
- a poster with a QR code

Anyone you send it to can open it in their browser. Nothing to download.

## Decide who can see it

You're always in control of who can open your page:

- **Anyone with the link** — great for a shop, an invite, or a portfolio you want out in the world.
- **Just the people you choose** — invite specific people by email when it's only for them.
- **Only you** — keep it private while you're still working on it.

You can change your mind any time — make a private page public when it's ready, or lock one back down.

## Ask for access

If someone sends you a link to a **private page** you're not on yet, ShareOut shows a
**Request access** button after you sign in. Tap it and the owner gets notified — in
ShareOut Home and, if they use it, on Telegram. They can approve with one click; you
then get **viewer** access without needing a new link.

You need to sign in with an **email address** (Google or email code) so the owner knows
who is asking. If you already requested access, the button shows that your request is
pending.

## It looks good when you share it

Paste your link into a text, WhatsApp, or Slack and it shows a **nice preview** — a title, a description, and a picture — instead of a bare web address. ShareOut makes that preview for you automatically, so your page looks polished the moment you send it. Want a specific picture or wording? Just ask.

## Updates are instant

Changed something? Everyone who has the link sees the new version right away. Same address, always current — no need to re-send anything.

:::tip[A nice touch]
Print your link as a **QR code** for a table tent, a flyer, or a shop window. People point their phone and they're on your page.
:::
