title: Change anything
description: Edit your page two ways — click a part and change it, or just ask the assistant. It saves as you go.
---

Once your page exists, nothing is locked in. You can change anything — the words, the colors, the photos, the order of things — in two easy ways.

## Way 1: Click it and change it

Open your page from [Home](/everyone/your-workspace/) and switch to **Edit**, or open
the full editor at `/a/{slug}/edit`. Click the part you want to change — a heading,
a button, a photo. A little set of options appears, and you can:

- **Rewrite the words** — just type over them.
- **Change colors and size** — pick from the options, no color codes to memorize.
- **Move things up or down**, or remove what you don't need.

It's point-and-change. If you can use a phone, you can do this.

## Way 2: Just ask

Prefer to talk? The assistant is right there on the side. Tell it what you want in plain words:

> *"Make the top section warmer and add a line about free local delivery."*
>
> *"Swap the blue buttons for green."*
>
> *"Add a section with three customer reviews."*

It makes the change and shows you the result. Big changes, small tweaks — either works.

## It saves as you go

You don't have to remember to save. Your changes are kept automatically as **your draft**. And if a change isn't quite right, you can **undo** it and try again — nothing is ever stuck.

A few things that help when you work with others:

- **Your draft is personal.** If a teammate edits the same page, each of you has your own draft when you reload. While you are both in the full editor, you can still see each other's live changes over the collab connection.
- **Publish makes it live.** When you publish, viewers get the version on **your** screen. Your draft clears; teammates keep theirs until they publish or discard.
- **Home quick Edit** is for fast text tweaks. Use **Open full editor** for data bindings, collaboration, version history, and the AI agent.

:::tip[Try both]
Most people end up mixing the two: ask the assistant for the big stuff ("add a contact form"), then click around to fine-tune the wording and colors yourself.
:::

:::note[AI assistant needs a key on self-host]
On a self-hosted instance the agent only works if the operator set an AI key (`ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`, `OPENAI_API_KEY`, or `VERCEL_AI_GATEWAY`). Click-to-edit and publish work either way.
:::
