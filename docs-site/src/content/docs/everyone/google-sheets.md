If you already keep things in a **Google Sheet** — a menu, a price list, a guest list, your stock — you can show it on your page and keep the two in sync. Edit the sheet, and the page follows.

## Why people love this

You probably already update a spreadsheet. Now that same spreadsheet can power a real, good-looking web page — with **zero double work**. Change a price in the sheet, and your live page shows the new price. No re-editing the page, ever.

## Connect it

1. On your page, choose to connect **Google Sheets**.
2. **Sign in with Google** to give permission (the usual "Continue with Google").
3. Pick your spreadsheet, and tell the page what to show — *"show the name, price, and photo from each row."*

That's it. Your list appears, pulled live from the sheet.

## Great for

- **A menu** that you update from your phone in the spreadsheet
- **A price list** the whole team can edit in one place
- **An inventory or "what's in stock"** page
- **An events or class calendar**
- **A directory** — members, team, vendors

## It stays fresh on its own

Add a row, fix a typo, mark something sold out — your page catches up. Want it to refresh on a set schedule (say, every morning)? See [**Put it on autopilot**](/everyone/automations/).

## Keep a page up to date from a spreadsheet

Already have the spreadsheet, and want a page's numbers to follow it? Open the page from Home, go to
**Automate → Update the data** (on a phone: **Options → Automate**), and pick one of three ways:

| If the file… | Choose | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| is private, in your Google Drive | **From Google Drive** | You pick it in Google's own window. ShareOut reads only that file, nothing else in your Drive. |
| is shared as "Anyone with the link" | **From a link** | You paste the link. No Google sign-in. |
| arrives by email, or lives on your computer | **By emailing the Excel** | You email the file to the page's address. See [Update your page's numbers by emailing the Excel](/everyone/inbox/#update-your-pages-numbers-by-emailing-the-excel). |

**From Google Drive:**

1. Press **Choose a spreadsheet**. A Google window opens: sign in if it asks, and pick the file.
2. Check the tab (the first one is used if you leave it empty) and when to update it (every day
   at 08:00 unless you change it).
3. Press **Save and sync now**. The page gets the rows right away and says so.

**From a link:**

1. In Google: **Share → General access → "Anyone with the link" → Viewer → Copy link**.
2. Paste it in **Share link** and press **Check link**. You see the tab and how many rows it has.
3. Pick when to update it and save.

:::caution[Anyone with the link can read the file]
Use a link only for data that can be public — never personal data or anything confidential. For a
private file, use **From Google Drive**: the file stays private.
:::

Good to know:

- The **first row** is the column titles. Each update **replaces** the rows the page shows.
- If the file hasn't changed since the last update, nothing is reloaded.
- Up to 8 MB and 100,000 rows per file.
- **If something fails**, the window says why in one sentence. The most common ones: *"This file
  isn't shared with 'Anyone with the link'"* (share it as in step 1 and check again) and *"Google
  Drive isn't set up on this instance yet"* (ask whoever runs your ShareOut to set it up).
- No page yet? **New → Table from an Excel file** makes the table and then offers these same three
  ways. See [Start from an Excel file](/everyone/inbox/#start-from-an-excel-file).

:::tip[Keep your sheet tidy]
Use a clear header row (like `Name`, `Price`, `Photo`) — it makes telling the page what to show much easier.
:::
