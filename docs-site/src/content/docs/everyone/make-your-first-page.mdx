Making a page feels less like "building a website" and more like asking for one.
You need a ShareOut to sign in to: ShareOut Cloud at [shareout.site](https://shareout.site)
(new accounts are by invitation for now) or your company's own. Setting one up
yourself? [Install guide](/self-host/overview/).

<Steps>

1. **Sign in to your instance.**

   Open shareout.site or your company's ShareOut address (ask your admin if you do
   not have it), and sign in or accept your invite.

2. **Describe what you want.**

   *Have an Excel or CSV file?* Pick **New → Table from an Excel file** instead: it
   turns the file into a clean table that reads well on a phone, then asks how to keep
   it up to date. [Start from an Excel file](/everyone/inbox/#start-from-an-excel-file).

   In Home, open **New** at the top of the left rail and pick **Describe it to AI**.
   Tell it, in your own words, what your page should be. Be as simple or as detailed as you like:

   > *"A page for my dog-walking business — what I offer, my prices, some photos, and a way for people to message me."*

   It may ask a question or two. Then it asks **Who can see it?** — leave **Private**
   for now — and you tap **Build it**. You watch the page come together in a new tab.
   If **Describe it to AI** isn't in the **New** menu, or nothing happens, the
   instance still needs an AI key — ask your admin (see the install Day 1 notes).

3. **Look it over — it's already published, just for you.**

   Your page appears with a real web address on this instance. No "publish" step to hunt for, no waiting. It starts **private**: only you can open it until you share it.

   New accounts also get a **starter kit** — example pages tucked in an **Examples**
   folder (kanban, polls, dashboards, and more) so Home never feels empty. Once you
   create your own work, examples fold away but stay in that folder and in search.

4. **Make it yours.**

   Want a different color? Friendlier wording? Move a photo up top? Just ask — *"make the heading bigger and warmer"* — or click the part you want and change it yourself. (More on that in [Change anything](/everyone/the-editor/).)

5. **Share the link.**

   Open the page and tap **Share**. Under **Who else can open it**, pick **Anyone with
   the link** (or invite people by email), then copy the address and send it anywhere —
   a text, an email, your Instagram bio. Until you do this, nobody else can open it.
   Don't see **Anyone with the link**? Your workspace doesn't allow public pages — ask
   your admin. (More in [Share it](/everyone/share-it/).)

</Steps>

## A tip for great results

The more you say, the closer it lands the first time. Instead of *"a shop page,"* try:

> *"A page to sell my homemade candles. Show three scents with photos and prices, a short story about how I make them, and a button to order on WhatsApp."*

You can always refine it after — but a good first description saves you a few rounds.
