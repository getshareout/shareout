title: What is ShareOut?
description: ShareOut turns a spreadsheet, a report or an idea into a page with its own link, and keeps it up to date — on shareout.site or your company's own ShareOut.
---

ShareOut turns an idea, a spreadsheet or a report into a real web page with its own
link. You (or an assistant like Claude or ChatGPT) describe what you want, and it
builds it. No code required.

You use it at [shareout.site](https://shareout.site), the hosted ShareOut (new
accounts are by invitation), or on your company's own ShareOut, which someone
[installs on their Cloudflare account](/self-host/overview/).

Think of it like texting a friend who happens to be great at making websites. You say:

> *"I need a page for my bakery — my menu, photos, and a little contact form."*

…and a few seconds later it's online on your instance, ready to share. From there
you can tweak anything yourself, drop in your own photos, and send it to the whole
world or just a few people.

## Why people love it

- **You talk, it builds.** Describe your page in plain words (**New → Describe it to AI**
  needs an AI key on the instance — ask your admin if that option does nothing).
- **It's live on its own link.** Every page gets a real web address you can send.
- **You're in control.** Don't like the color? The wording? Move something? Just
  say so, or click and change it yourself.
- **It grows with you.** Start with a simple page. Add photos, a form, or live
  data from Sheets whenever you're ready.

## What it's *not*

It's not a website builder full of menus and settings. And you don't need to be a
developer to use it.

If you can describe what you want, you can make it.

## Words you'll see

- **Page** or **artifact** — anything you make in ShareOut: a table, a report, a
  dashboard, a small website. It opens in the browser and has its own link.
- **Workspace** — your team's space in ShareOut, with its folders, people and pages.
- **Data** (or **dataset**) — the rows a page shows, like the table from your Excel.
  When the file changes, the page can update itself.
- **Connector** — a link to a tool you already use (Google Sheets, your store, Claude
  or ChatGPT) so ShareOut can read from it or work with it.
- **Share** — choose who can open a page: anyone with the link, your team, or only
  the people you pick.
