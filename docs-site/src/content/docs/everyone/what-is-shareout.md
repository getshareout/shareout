---
title: What is ShareOut?
description: On your self-hosted ShareOut, describe a page and it goes live — for your team, on your Cloudflare account.
---

ShareOut turns an idea into a real web page on **your** instance. You (or an agent)
describe what you want, and it builds it — live, with its own link. No code required
to use the product once someone has [installed ShareOut](/self-host/overview/).

There is no ShareOut cloud to sign up for. Someone on your team deploys the Worker
to Cloudflare; then people open **your** URL and get to work.

Think of it like texting a friend who happens to be great at making websites. You say:

> *"I need a page for my bakery — my menu, photos, and a little contact form."*

…and a few seconds later it's online on your instance, ready to share. From there
you can tweak anything yourself, drop in your own photos, and send it to the whole
world or just a few people.

## Why people love it

- **You talk, it builds.** Describe your page in plain words (Create with AI needs
  an AI key on the instance — ask your admin if that button does nothing).
- **It's live on your link.** Every page gets a real address on *your* domain or
  workers.dev URL.
- **You're in control.** Don't like the color? The wording? Move something? Just
  say so, or click and change it yourself.
- **It grows with you.** Start with a simple page. Add photos, a form, or live
  data from Sheets whenever you're ready.

## What it's *not*

It's not a website builder full of menus and settings. It's not “install nothing
and use our cloud” — the product is **self-hosted**. And you don't need to be a
developer to *use* it after it's up.

If you can describe what you want, you can make it.
