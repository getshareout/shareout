Some pages are really a **list**: products, events, team members, FAQs, prices. ShareOut can show that list beautifully and let you update it whenever — without redoing the page.

## Add a list

Describe what the list holds:

> *"A list of my products, each with a name, price, and photo."*
>
> *"An events list with a title, date, and short description."*
>
> *"A team page with each person's name, role, and photo."*

The page shows it nicely — a grid of cards, a table, whatever fits.

## Update it any time

Add a new product, change a price, remove a sold-out item — your page updates to match. No need to re-describe the whole page; you're just editing the list.

## Where the list can come from

- **You type it in** — perfect for a small, hand-kept list.
- **A spreadsheet you connect** — keep your info in Google Sheets and the page follows along. (See [Connect your tools](/everyone/connect/).)
- **Form replies** — the things people submit can become a list too (a sign-up sheet, an order log).

## Good for

- A menu or product catalog
- An events or class calendar
- A team or members directory
- A price list or service menu
- An FAQ

:::tip[Keep it fresh on its own]
Connect a spreadsheet and your list updates itself whenever you edit the sheet — great for a menu or a "what's in stock" page. More in [Connect your tools](/everyone/connect/).
:::

## Share a list between pages

On a **team workspace**, one page can keep a list and let your *other* pages use it. A sign-up page collects names; your dashboard page shows them — same list, no copying.

The page that owns the list decides whether the others can **just read** it or also **add to it**. Everyone on the team can see what's been shared under the **Datasets** tab in your workspace home.

> *"Share this sign-ups list with my workspace so my dashboard page can show it."*
