You don't have to do everything yourself. Invite people to your page and choose what each of them can do.

## Invite by email

Add someone with their email address and pick what they're allowed to do:

- **Can edit** — they can change and update the page with you. Great for a teammate, a partner, or a co-organizer.
- **Can view** — they can see it (even if it's private), but not change it. Great for a client reviewing, or a boss signing off.

You're always the **owner** — only you can delete the page or hand it over to someone else.

**Workspace owners and admins** can also edit any page in the workspace (Live Studio
and quick Edit on Home) even without a named collaborator invite, and they manage
who a page is shared with — the invite list, handing over ownership, and the access
queue — for any page in their workspace. That's what keeps a page reshareable after
the person who made it leaves. Plain workspace **members** still need **Can edit** on
each page they don't own.

Unresolved comments on your pages appear in **Needs You** on [Home](/everyone/your-workspace/).
You can reply from the page viewer or the **Comments** tab in the Inspector without
opening the full editor.

## Access requests

When someone opens your private page but isn't on the invite list, they can tap
**Request access** after signing in. Pending requests show up as a banner on
**Home** — approve or deny right there. If you use the
[Telegram bot](/guides/telegram-bot/), you also get inline **Approve / Deny** buttons
in chat.

Approving adds them as a **viewer** collaborator automatically. Denying leaves the
page private with no access granted, and the same person can't ask again for a week —
so a "no" stays a no instead of pinging you on repeat. Workspace owners and admins
see and can answer requests for any page in their workspace, not just their own.

## Who can do what

| | You (owner) | Can edit | Can view |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See the page | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Change & update it | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Invite others | ✓ | ✓ | — |
| Delete it | ✓ | — | — |

## Handy for

- **A small team** building a page together
- **A client or boss** who needs to see a private draft before it goes out
- **An event** where a few organizers all keep the page current
- **Handing off** — make someone else the owner when a project changes hands

:::tip[Private but shared]
Keep a page **private** and invite just the people who need it — perfect for a draft, an internal page, or something only your team should see. More in [Share it](/everyone/share-it/).
:::

:::tip[Formal client sharing on Teams]
Need a grouped portal, read receipts, scoped API, or notes your assistant
remembers? On Teams, use [External sharing](/teams/external-sharing/) instead of
one-off email invites on each page.
:::
