## Your idea, live on *your* instance

ShareOut is **self-hosted only**. Someone deploys it to Cloudflare; then you
describe a page, it builds it, and you get a link on your domain.

<CardGrid stagger>
	<Card title="Install first">
		Clone the repo, `npm run deploy`, open `/setup`. [Install guide](/self-host/overview/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Just describe it">
		Tell ShareOut what you want — *"a page for my bakery with my menu and a contact form"* — and it builds it. [Make your first page](/everyone/make-your-first-page/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Make it yours">
		Add photos, change words and colors, drop in a form. Click or ask. [The editor](/everyone/the-editor/).
	</Card>
	<Card title="Share with anyone">
		Every page gets its own link. Whole world, or a few people. [Share it](/everyone/share-it/).
	</Card>
</CardGrid>
